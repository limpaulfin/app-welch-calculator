APP-Welch Calculator

Sample size for two-group means with unequal variances.

Inputs (drag slider or type a number)

By APP design, $n_{\min}$ depends only on $f$ and $c$. The variance ratio changes $\nu^*$, $t$-critical, and the CI half-width — drag $\sigma_1$ or $\sigma_2$ to see those move while $n_{\min}$ stays fixed.

Formulas

$$n_{\min} = \left\lceil \left(\frac{z_{(1+c)/2}}{f}\right)^{\!2} \right\rceil \text{ per group.}$$

$$\nu^* = \frac{(\sigma_1^2/n + \sigma_2^2/n)^2}{\dfrac{(\sigma_1^2/n)^2}{n-1} + \dfrac{(\sigma_2^2/n)^2}{n-1}} \quad (\text{Satterthwaite}).$$

$$\text{CI half-width} = t_{\nu^{*},\,(1+c)/2}\, \sqrt{\dfrac{\sigma_1^2}{n} + \dfrac{\sigma_2^2}{n}}.$$

$z$ via Beasley-Springer-Moro. $t$ via Newton on Lentz incomplete beta.
