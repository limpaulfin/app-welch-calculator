APP-Welch Calculator

Sample size for two-group means with unequal variances.

Inputs

Results (per-group balanced design)

Formulas

nmin = ⌈(z(1+c)/2 / f)²⌉ per group.

ν* = (σ²1/n + σ²2/n)² / [(σ²1/n)²/(n−1) + (σ²2/n)²/(n−1)] (Satterthwaite).

CI half-width = tν*, (1+c)/2 · √(σ²1/n + σ²2/n).

z uses Beasley-Springer-Moro. t uses Newton iteration on the Lentz incomplete beta.

What it does

Type the precision f, the assurance c, and the two group standard deviations. The calculator returns the minimum sample per group, the Welch effective degrees of freedom, and the confidence interval half-width. All math runs in the browser. No server. No tracking.
