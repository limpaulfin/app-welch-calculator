APP-Welch Sample Size Calculator

A Priori Procedure under Heterogeneous Variances. Welch-Satterthwaite extension.

Inputs

Results (per-group balanced design)

Formulas

nmin = ⌈(z(1+c)/2 / f)²⌉ per group.

ν* = (σ²1/n + σ²2/n)² / [(σ²1/n)²/(n−1) + (σ²2/n)²/(n−1)] (Satterthwaite).

CI half-width = tν*, (1+c)/2 · √(σ²1/n + σ²2/n).

z computed via Beasley-Springer-Moro inverse normal. t computed via Newton iteration on Lentz incomplete beta.

Methodological Background

The A Priori Procedure was introduced by Trafimow & MacDonald (2017, doi:10.1177/0013164416659745) and further developed by Wang, Wang & Trafimow (2019), Trafimow et al. (2020), and Trafimow (2023, doi:10.4324/9781003365167-7). Heteroscedastic inference uses the Welch (1947) t-statistic with Satterthwaite (1946) effective degrees of freedom. Online calculators accompanying earlier APP papers are linked from the Trafimow (2023) chapter.
